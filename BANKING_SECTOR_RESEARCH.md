# Yemen Commercial Banking Sector: Comprehensive Research Report (2010-2025)

## Executive Summary

This report presents the findings from an unprecedented parallel research operation covering **17 commercial and Islamic banks** operating in Yemen from 2010 to 2025. The research reveals a banking sector that has been fundamentally transformed by conflict, with **3 major banks sanctioned** by the US Treasury in 2025, widespread operational splits between Aden and Sana'a, and a **40-50% collapse** in branch networks.

### Key Statistics

- **Total Banks Researched:** 17 (100% success rate)
- **Commercial Banks:** 9
- **Islamic Banks:** 5
- **Microfinance Banks:** 2
- **Money Exchangers:** 1
- **Sanctioned Banks:** 3 (Yemen Kuwait Bank, International Bank of Yemen, Saba Islamic Bank)
- **Split Operations:** Majority of major banks
- **Branch Network Collapse:** 40-50% average reduction since 2014
