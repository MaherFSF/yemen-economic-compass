# CBY-Aden Circular #4/2025 - Enhanced Due Diligence & International Sanctions Compliance

**Issuer:** Central Bank of Yemen - Head Office Aden  
**Date:** 02/11/2025 (November 2, 2025)  
**Reference:** Circular #4/2025  
**Subject:** Instructions for Implementing Enhanced Due Diligence Procedures and Verification Against International Sanctions Lists

## Addressed To
All banks, money exchange companies, and financial institutions operating in the Republic

## Legal Basis
- Law #1/2010 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating Terrorism Financing
- CBY Circular #1/2012 and #1/2013 on anti-money laundering instructions
- CBY Decision dated 28/06/2020 on linking with international sanctions lists
- FATF (Financial Action Task Force) requirements
- UN Security Council resolutions on foreign assets monitoring (OFAC)

## Objectives

1. **Strengthen compliance** with anti-money laundering and terrorism financing laws through enhanced due diligence and international sanctions list verification
2. **Protect the financial sector** from risks of money laundering and terrorism financing, in line with FATF guidelines and UN Security Council resolutions
3. **Promote cooperation** between banks/financial institutions and regulatory authorities to detect and prevent illegal financial activities
4. **Establish principles** of transparency and integrity in the banking system, protecting its reputation and ensuring customer trust

## Key Requirements

### Mandatory Sanctions Lists Screening
All banks and financial institutions MUST screen against:
- **UN Sanctions List**
- **OFAC (Office of Foreign Assets Control)** - US Treasury
- **EU Sanctions List**
- **HMT (Her Majesty's Treasury)** - UK sanctions
- **Any local or regional lists** issued by CBY or national authorities

### Enhanced Due Diligence Systems
Banks must implement specialized technical screening systems, including:
- **Fircosoft**
- **Accuity (Refinitiv World-Check)**
- **Dow Jones Risk & Compliance**
- **LexisNexis Bridger Insight**
- Or any other system approved by CBY

### System Requirements
Screening systems must include:
- **Automatic updates** with local and international sanctions lists
- **Integration with core banking systems**
- **Arabic and English language support** for internal and external use
- **Fuzzy matching** capabilities for Arabic and foreign names
- **Detailed audit reports** for each screening operation

### Operational Procedures

1. **Appoint Sanctions Officer** - Dedicated personnel responsible for sanctions compliance, system operation, and periodic reporting

2. **Establish policies and procedures** including:
   - Manual and automated screening steps
   - Escalation mechanisms for matches
   - Internal and external reporting channels
   - Record retention for all screening operations

3. **Maintain comprehensive records** of all screening operations, matches, and actions taken

4. **Prohibition on transactions** with any individual or entity appearing on sanctions lists, with immediate implementation of legal procedures

5. **Immediate suspension** of accounts and freezing of funds upon positive match, pending further investigation

6. **False positive handling** - Establish procedures for investigating and clearing false matches with sanctioned entities

7. **Continuous monitoring** - Suspend transactions until verification is complete when similarities are detected

### Reporting Requirements

Banks must submit quarterly reports to CBY including:
- Number of screenings conducted
- Number of positive matches and actions taken
- Operational or technical challenges encountered

### Compliance Timeline

- **Detailed quarterly reports** - Every 30 days from circular issuance
- **Final implementation deadline** - 90 days from circular issuance for full compliance

## Enforcement

Non-compliance will result in:
- Regulatory sanctions
- Operational restrictions
- Potential license revocation

## Significance

This circular represents CBY-Aden's commitment to:
- International banking standards compliance
- Protection of Yemen's financial system reputation
- Prevention of illicit financial flows
- Alignment with global AML/CFT frameworks
