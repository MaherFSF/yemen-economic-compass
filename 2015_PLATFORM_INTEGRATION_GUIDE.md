# Yemen Financial Observatory - 2015 Comprehensive Integration Guide

## Executive Summary

This document synthesizes all 2015 research findings across 12 economic sectors and 17 financial institutions to guide the comprehensive update of the entire Yemen Financial Observatory platform. The year 2015 marked the catastrophic onset of Yemen's conflict (March 26, 2015), triggering the worst humanitarian crisis in the world and a complete economic collapse.

---

## I. MACROECONOMIC INDICATORS 2015

### GDP & Economic Contraction
- **GDP (current US$)**: $24.819 billion
- **GDP Growth Rate**: **-36.1%** (catastrophic contraction)
- **GDP per capita**: $870.3
- **Real GDP per capita decline since 2015**: **-58%**

### Inflation & Currency
- **Annual Inflation Rate**: **22.00%**
- **Official Exchange Rate**: **215.11 YER/USD** (maintained throughout 2015)
- **CBY Foreign Reserves (late 2014)**: $4.6 billion
- **Reserves Spent (H1 2015)**: $1.84 billion (40% of total)

### Trade Balance
- **Exports**: $510 million
- **Imports**: $6.573 billion
- **Trade Balance**: **-$6.063 billion** (massive deficit)

---

## II. CENTRAL BANKING CRISIS 2015

### Central Bank of Yemen (Unified - Pre-Split)
**Key Events:**
- **March 2015**: Conflict escalation triggers CBY crisis
- **H1 2015**: CBY spends 40% of foreign reserves ($1.84B) on essential imports
- **Throughout 2015**: Official exchange rate maintained at 215 YER/USD
- **2015**: Cash distributions reduced to cover only basic operating expenses

**IMF Engagement:**
- **September 29, 2015**: IMF authorizes SDR 95.2 million (~$121M) disbursement
- **March 26, 2015**: IMF suspends $552.9M Extended Credit Facility due to war

**Financial Metrics:**
- Foreign reserves depleted from $4.6B to ~$2.76B by end of 2015
- Monetary policy effectively paralyzed
- Government revenues collapsed

---

## III. COMMERCIAL BANKING SECTOR 2015

### Sector-Wide Crisis
- **Total Banking System Assets**: ~YER 3.4 trillion
- **Commercial Bank Assets Frozen at CBY**: YER 1.78 trillion
- **Consolidated Deposits (January 2015)**: YER 2,197,537.40 million
- **Non-Performing Loan (NPL) Ratio**: **23.3%** of gross loans
- **Crisis Impact**: Banking operations effectively halted after March 2015

### Individual Bank Performance 2015

#### 1. CAC Bank (Cooperative & Agricultural Credit Bank)
- **Total Assets**: YER 359.163 billion
- **Customer Deposits**: YER 309.578 billion
- **Net Loans**: YER 38.139 billion
- **Gross NPLs**: YER 17.674 billion
- **NPL Provision**: YER 17.398 billion
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio**: 39.6%
- **Liquidity Ratio**: 83.73% (vs 25% CBY minimum)
- **Branches**: 49 (down from 53 in 2014)
- **Auditor**: Grant Thornton Yemen
- **Audit Approval**: June 1, 2016

#### 2. Yemen Kuwait Bank (YKB)
- **Total Assets**: YER 121.8 billion (+26% from 2014)
- **Customer Deposits**: YER 107.9 billion (+24% from 2014)
- **Net Loans**: YER 18.4 billion (down from YER 20B in 2014)
- **Net Loss**: **YER 333.5 million** (vs YER 405.9M profit in 2014)
- **Debt Provision Deficit**: YER 1.4 billion
- **Capital Adequacy Ratio**: 21.12%
- **Key Impact**: Sharp profitability reversal due to NPL surge and branch closures

## V. MONEY EXCHANGE & REMITTANCES SECTOR 2015

### Sector Expansion
- **Total Remittances to Yemen**: **$3.3 billion** (primary source of foreign currency)
- **Economic Contraction**: **-33%**
- **Number of Money Exchange Entities (Feb 20, 2015)**: 551
- **Licensed Money Exchange Companies**: 38
- **Remittance Operations Processed**: 767 (2015)

**Key Shift**: Money exchange sector became de facto backbone of Yemen's economy, replacing collapsed formal banking system for trade financing and remittances.

## VI. HUMANITARIAN CRISIS & AID FLOWS 2015

### Overall Humanitarian Situation
- **People Requiring Assistance**: 11.7 million (by end of 2015)
- **Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs)**: 2.5 million (as of November 30, 2015)
- **Refugees/Migrants Departed Yemen**: 169,863 (since March 2015)
- **Conflict Casualties (March-Oct 2015)**: 5,604 deaths, 26,703 injuries
- **Total Fatalities 2015 (all parties)**: ~17,100
- **Civilian Fatalities (direct targeting)**: ~4,500

### Yemen Humanitarian Response Plan (YHRP) 2015
- **Total Requirement**: $1.6 billion
- **Contributions Received (as of Sept 30)**: $732 million (46% funded)
- **Total Incoming Funding (OCHA FTS)**: $1.76 billion

## VII. DONOR & STAKEHOLDER OPERATIONS 2015

### 1. World Food Programme (WFP)
- **Beneficiaries Reached**: **8.9 million people**
- **Funding Received**: **$359.7 million** (20.4% of total humanitarian funding)
- **Emergency Food Assistance (October 2015)**: 2,803,000 people, 30,700 MT
- **Key Transition**: PRRO 200636 → EMOP 200890 (October 2015-March 2016)
- **Staff Evacuation**: 4-month evacuation of international staff (March-July 2015)

### 2. UNICEF
- **Initial Appeal**: $60.1 million
- **Revised Appeal (post-conflict)**: $182.6 million
- **Estimated Funding Received**: ~$137 million (75% of revised appeal)
- **Children Under 5 Deaths (preventable diseases)**: Nearly 10,000
- **Children with Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM)**: 160,629
- **People Lacking Safe Water**: 13.4 million
- **Children Affected by School Attacks/Closures**: 200,000
- **GPE Education Grant**: $10 million

### 3. United Arab Emirates (UAE)
- **Total Aid**: AED 485 million (**$132.03 million**)
- **Share of Total Aid**: **31%** (largest donor in 2015)
- **Sector Allocation**:
  * Electric Power: AED 170M ($46.27M)
  * Food: AED 131M ($35.67M)
  * Medical Supplies: AED 73M ($19.87M)
  * Relief Items: AED 58M ($15.79M)
- **WASH Project (UAE Red Crescent)**: $2,066,433

### 4. Saudi Arabia
- **Total Humanitarian Funding**: **$322,305,356**
- **Military Operations**:
  * Operation Decisive Storm: March 26 - April 21, 2015
  * Operation Restoring Hope: April 22, 2015 onwards
- **Civilian Casualties (Saudi-led coalition)**: ~67% of total direct targeting deaths
- **Food Distribution**: 100,000 parcels → 600,000 beneficiaries (Aden/Lahj)

### 5. World Health Organization (WHO)
- **2015 Response Plan**: $83 million requested
- **Funding Received**: $36.8 million (44% funded, 56% gap)
- **Beneficiaries (Taiz)**: 600,000 (30 MT medical supplies)
- **Safe Water Delivered (Taiz)**: 1 million liters
- **Conflict Casualties Reported (March 19-Oct 16)**: 5,604 deaths, 26,703 injuries

### 6. UNHCR
- **IDPs Validated**: 2,509,062 (December 2015)
- **Refugees/Migrants Departed**: 169,863 (since March 2015)
- **Funding Request**: $153 million
- **Funding Gap**: 56%

### 7. UNDP
- **Yemen National Dialogue & Constitutional Reform Trust Fund (YNDCRTF)**:
  * Total Contributions: $25.22 million
  * Total Expenditures: $22.41 million (99% of allocated funds)
- **Key Achievement**: Draft Constitution completed January 5, 2015
- **Crisis Impact**: Process halted February 2015, Secretariat closed September 2015

### 8. FAO
- **Food Insecure Population (November 2015)**: **14.4 million** (50% of population, +36% from 2014)
- **Level 3 Emergency Response**: Activated July 14, 2015
- **Funding Request**: $19 million
- **Funding Received**: $14.1 million (74% funded)
- **Direct Beneficiaries**: Over 60,000 people

### 9. World Bank
- **Sana'a Office Closure**: February 18, 2015
- **Operations Suspension**: **March 11, 2015** (all IDA and trust fund projects)
- **Reason**: Inability to communicate with government, ensure fiduciary oversight

### 10. International Monetary Fund (IMF)
- **Extended Credit Facility (ECF) Suspension**: **March 26, 2015**
- **ECF Amount**: $552.9 million (approved September 2014)
- **SDR Assessment Due (April 30, 2015)**: 1,563 SDR
- **Cumulative GDP Contraction (2015-2017)**: -24%

## IX. KEY 2015 EVENTS TIMELINE

### January-February 2015
- **January 5, 2015**: Draft Constitution completed (UNDP YNDCRTF)
- **February 18, 2015**: World Bank temporarily closes Sana'a office
- **February 20, 2015**: 551 money exchangers and 38 licensed companies operating

### March 2015 (Conflict Escalation)
- **March 11, 2015**: World Bank suspends all operations in Yemen
- **March 26, 2015**: 
  * Saudi-led Operation Decisive Storm begins
  * IMF suspends $552.9M Extended Credit Facility
  * Conflict triggers humanitarian catastrophe
- **March-July 2015**: WFP evacuates international staff for 4 months

### April-June 2015
- **April 21, 2015**: Operation Decisive Storm ends
- **April 22, 2015**: Operation Restoring Hope begins
- **April 30, 2015**: IMF SDR assessment payment due (1,563 SDR)
- **H1 2015**: CBY spends $1.84B (40% of reserves) on essential imports

### July-September 2015
- **July 14, 2015**: FAO activates Level 3 Emergency Response
- **September 29, 2015**: IMF authorizes SDR 95.2M disbursement (~$121M)
- **September 30, 2015**: YHRP 46% funded ($732M of $1.6B)

### October-December 2015
- **October 2015**: WFP launches EMOP 200890, assists 2.8M people with 30,700 MT food
- **November 30, 2015**: 2.5M IDPs, 11.7M people need assistance
- **December 31, 2015**: 
  * WFP PRRO 200636 officially closes
  * AMB active borrowers: 37,678, portfolio: YER 2.4B
  * CAC Bank: 49 branches, YER 359.163B assets
  * YKB: Net loss YER 333.5M
