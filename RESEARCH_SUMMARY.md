## Coverage Breakdown

### Temporal Coverage (16 Years)

#### Pre-Crisis Period (2010-2014)
- **2010:** Economic fragility with 7.7% GDP growth but high inflation (9.8%) and widespread poverty (46.6%)
- **2011:** Arab Spring revolution caused severe economic shock, GDP contracted by 12.0%, inflation surged to 19.5%
- **2012:** Modest economic rebound (2.4% GDP growth) with $8 billion donor support
- **2013:** Fragile political transition, 6.0% GDP growth but high unemployment (13.67%)
- **2014:** Houthi takeover triggered by fuel subsidy removal, poverty rate reached 49%

#### War Period (2015-2019)
- **2015:** Operation Decisive Storm, GDP contracted by 28%, humanitarian crisis began
- **2016:** Central Bank split created dual economy, hyperinflation (254.9%), poverty reached 70.9%
- **2017:** Cholera outbreak (450,000 cases), 37.5% cumulative GDP contraction
- **2018:** Currency crisis, exchange rate collapsed to 800 YER/USD, 22.2 million in need
- **2019:** Riyadh Agreement, Houthi currency ban, 24.1 million needing aid

#### Recent Crisis (2020-2025)
- **2020:** COVID-19 impact, 8.5% GDP contraction, remittances declined
- **2021:** Fuel crisis, 72% decline in Hodeidah fuel imports, IMF SDR allocation ($660M)
- **2022:** UN truce provided economic breathing space, $2B Saudi/UAE pledge
- **2023:** Truce collapse, Red Sea crisis begins, 2.0% GDP contraction
- **2024:** Exchange rate crisis (1,917 YER/USD in Aden), 18.2 million need aid
- **2025:** Projected 0.5-1.5% GDP contraction, humanitarian plan only 19% funded

## Key Findings by Theme

### Economic Fragmentation
The 2016 Central Bank split created two parallel economies with severe consequences:
- **Currency Divergence:** 26% exchange rate difference (2016), widening to 68% (2024)
- **Dual Monetary Policy:** Competing policies exacerbating inflation
- **Fiscal Fragmentation:** Separate revenue collection and expenditure systems

### Humanitarian Crisis
Progressive deterioration of humanitarian situation:
- **People in Need:** 10M (2012) → 24.1M (2019) → 19M+ (2025)
- **Food Insecurity:** 10M (2012) → 17M+ (2024)
- **IDP Population:** 416K (2011) → 4.8M (2024)
- **Poverty Rate:** 46.6% (2010) → 70.9% (2016) → estimated 80%+ (2024)

### GDP Collapse
Cumulative economic destruction:
- **2015:** -28% (war begins)
- **2011-2017:** -37.5% cumulative
- **2015-2024:** -54% to -58% real GDP per capita decline
- **2025 Projection:** Additional -0.5% to -1.5%

### Aid Dependency & Shortfalls
Critical gap between needs and funding:
- **2012:** $8B pledged by Friends of Yemen
- **2018:** $2B Saudi deposit for currency stabilization
- **2022:** $2B Saudi/UAE pledge during truce
- **2025:** $2.5B humanitarian plan only 19-24% funded

### Causation Relationships
Major cause-effect chains identified:
1. **Fuel Subsidy Removal (2014) → Houthi Takeover → War**
2. **CBY Split (2016) → Currency Fragmentation → Hyperinflation → Poverty**
3. **War → GDP Collapse → Humanitarian Crisis → Aid Dependency**
4. **Oil Export Blockade → Fiscal Crisis → Exchange Rate Collapse**
5. **Red Sea Attacks → Import Costs → Inflation → Food Insecurity**
