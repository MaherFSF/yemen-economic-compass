- Humanitarian crisis escalates
- Economic collapse accelerates

### 2016: CBY Split - CRITICAL YEAR
- **September 18, 2016: Central Bank split** (most important event)
- CBY moved from Sana'a to Aden
- Dual monetary systems begin
- Exchange rate divergence starts
- Banking sector fragmentation

### 2017-2019: Dual Systems Consolidate
- Exchange rate gap widens

- Sana'a: maintains ~600 YER/USD
- Humanitarian crisis deepens
- Microfinance sector grows
- International aid increases

- COVID-19 pandemic impact

- Currency crisis intensifies
- Food insecurity peaks
- Banking sector stress

### 2022-2023: Truce & Aftermath
- April 2022: UN-brokered truce

- Economic stabilization attempts
- CBY reunification discussions
- Exchange rate volatility

### 2024-2025: Current Period
