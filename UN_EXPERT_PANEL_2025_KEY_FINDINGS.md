# UN Expert Panel on Yemen - 2025 Report Key Findings

**Document:** S/2025/650
**Date:** 17 October 2025
**Source:** UN Security Council

## Executive Summary

The UN Panel of Experts on Yemen submitted their final report pursuant to Security Council Resolution 2758 (2024), covering the period from August 1, 2024 to July 31, 2025.

## Key Findings

### Conflict Dynamics
- Continued deterioration of the situation in Yemen despite ongoing peace efforts
- Over a decade of continuous conflict with no end in sight
- Houthis continue to pose significant threats to regional peace and security

### Arms Embargo Violations
- Persistent violations of the UN arms embargo
- Continued flow of weapons to conflict parties
- Evidence of sophisticated weapons systems reaching Houthi forces

### Financial Networks
- Houthis maintain complex financial networks to sustain operations
- Revenue generation through various illicit means
- International financial system exploitation

### Humanitarian Impact
- Severe humanitarian crisis affecting millions
- Restricted humanitarian access in conflict zones
- Continued targeting of civilian infrastructure

### Sanctions Effectiveness
- Challenges in implementing and enforcing sanctions
- Need for enhanced monitoring and compliance mechanisms
- Gaps in international coordination on sanctions enforcement
